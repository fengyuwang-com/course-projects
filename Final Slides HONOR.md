## **Privacy Enthusiast YOYO** 

**Generated by Doubao AI** 

**1. Privacy Issues:** The phone and YOYO may collect lots of user data. Consumers may worry about privacy issues. **Solutions: Data Processing Visualization:** Offer an intuitive interface to show how user data is handled and stored. **Prefer Offline Data Processing:** Keep sensitive data processing on devices. **User Permissions and Data Control:** Provide options for users to manage data access permissions and delete privacy-related data. 

## **Potential Challenges & Solutions** 

**2. Acceptance challenge:** Middleaged and elderly people might find it difficult to adapt to or fully understand some ads or functions for young people. **Solutions** : **Targeted ads Provide different interaction methods for different age groups** 

**3. Understanding Barriers:** Consumers lack awareness of HONOR’s uniqueness such as AI. **Solutions** : **Interactive Demonstration Platform:** Dynamic feature showcases via website or app to reduce barriers. **Online and offline courses and** . **workshop**
