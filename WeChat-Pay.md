# **Social Media Marketing** 

## **Social Media Marketing** 

**"WeChat Pay Moments Incentive Program"** A social media campaign combining UGC (user generated content) and PGC (professional generated content) will be launched on the WeChat platform. The core of the campaign is to encourage users to share their financial management stories through WeChat Moments and official accounts, while a professional team provides authoritative content to enhance trust. 

Activity mechanism: Users post pictures or short videos with the #WeChatPay financial management tag in Moments. 

WeChat Pay official account collaborates with financial experts, KOLs and partner financial institutions to regularly publish professional content on different platforms (Rednote/ Tik Tok). 

Brand implantation: The "safe, professional and convenient" brand message of WeChat Pay's financial management is integrated into the event. 

Target audience: Mainly urban white-collar and middle-class users aged 25-40. 

## Timeline 

## **Month 1-2: Event preparation and PGC development** 

Design UGC contribution mechanism and miniprograms (rankings, financial knowledge). Cooperate with financial experts and KOLs to produce short videos to introduce Zero-Balance Wallet and funds. Publish event notices on WeChat Pay official accounts and push 1-2 PGC short videos. 

## **Month 3-4: UGC launch and PGC deepening** 

Encourage users to share #WeChatPay financial content, select 5 UGCs every week, forward and attach PGC expert comments. PGC content output: Publish 1-2 short videos and 1 picture and text per week, and 1 video account live broadcast per month (expert answers + product promotion). Adjust rewards based on mini-program data and optimize PGC content style. 

## **Month 5-6: Event climax and ending** 

Launch the "Best Financial Story" selection, users vote for the best UGC of the month, and issue fund share rewards. Hold large-scale live broadcasts to promote financial products. Release summary videos and push "Financial Trend Report". Announce event results and archive content for subsequent marketing.
