## **Testing Method for Prototype 1** 

## **GOAL** 

To understand whether AI makes target users more willing to sort waste; Find out if AI makes garbage collection easier for target users. 

## **TEST METHOD** 

Field Concept Testing: Covert Observation + In App Survey 

## **PROCEDURE** 

**Deploy interface** in Wan Chai recycling station **Informed consent** with users before (That their reactions may be recorded) Let target users **freely interact** with it, ask any questions they have about waste sorting using the prototype. 

**Observe and record** the user behaviour via camera, reactions, and questions while using the prototype. 

Give the target users a **user feedback survey** in the chat box to evaluate the prototype on various aspects and provide comments and suggestions 

## **JUSTIFICATION** 

**Covert Observation:** Capturing real, natuaral reactions (i.e Confused, puzzled, excited...etc.) high experimental validity. **In App Survey:** Convenient and detailed address of whether they’ve found it useful and been educated.
